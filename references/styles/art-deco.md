# Style: Art Deco  - source folder `art-deco`

Paraphrased from lemo-opuscar `styles/art-deco/STYLE.md` + `DEMO.md` (MIT). Not copied verbatim.

**One-liner:** a 1930s deco hotel world in gold, lacquer black and one burgundy hero: symmetric compositions, stepped arches and sunbursts, gold line-work that draws on to the beat, light counted bulb by bulb.
**Good for:** luxury/brand stories, "grand hotel" or cinema-palace tales, big reveals, finance/history with glamour. Indian angle: Mumbai's Marine Drive/Eros Cinema deco, old picture-palaces, Bombay deco hotels.

## Palette (write six-digit hex; three-digit shorthand shifts emerald toward violet)
Ground warm black `#0d0b09`, lacquer `#060504`, night sky `#0a0e16` fading to horizon emerald `#10231f`. Gold ramp: `#6F5220` shadow, `#C9A24B` base, `#F3D98B` light, `#FFF4D2` specular. Accents: burgundy `#8E1B2E` (the protagonist, the only large saturated area), emerald `#1D6B57`, ivory `#F2E8D5` (paper, gloves, type).

## Type (all OFL)
Limelight for titles, Poiret One for numbers/sign skeletons/plaques, Josefin Sans for subtitles and labels (SemiBold ~40-42 px, ivory on an ~88% black lacquer bar), Italiana for numerals. Subtitle hold >= max(1.8 s, speech+0.6 s).

## Materials
- Gold is a banded metal gradient; animate a `sheen` parameter for a light sweep. Signature keyline: a dark engraved underline ~1.6 px wider than the gold line plus a hairline highlight.
- Volumes are hard-edged with an airbrushed gradient and a thin gold rim on the lit side; light from upper left; true one- or two-point perspective.
- Gold line draws at the speed of the phrase under it and lands on the hit.

## Composition
The centre axis is sacred: every composition is symmetric about it (an asymmetric frame must resolve to it within the shot); everything unfolds from the centre. Titles in a stepped gold bar over an arch or sunburst; stepped arches frame, sunbursts sit behind, dials carry plot numbers.

## Motion
Characters step on twos (12 fps); camera, light, bulbs, gold draw-on and sheen on ones (24 fps). Bulb ignition overshoots ~1.6x and settles in ~0.18 s followed by a relay "chunk"; mechanical easing is firm ease-in-out with a small settle. Vocabulary: symmetric split (door leaves hinged at the seam), revolving-door wipe, extreme low-angle tilt, architectural section (cutaway pull-out), Busby-Berkeley overhead (mirrored, rotating), push-in on a dial/clock, match cut on shape, low oblique along a marquee lighting letter by letter, pull back from detail to skyline. Count light: e.g. twelve bells light twelve letters one by one. Use true silence before a big beat.

## Avoid
Gold-foil slideshow (things must move and have volume), Art Nouveau curves/flowers, flat vector poster, 60s spy silhouettes on flat colour, any black cartoon outline, generic dissolves/slides, drifting off the centre axis, pasted generic cartoon characters (characters must be deco-styled), fish-scale patterns that read as brickwork.

## Sound palette
Foxtrot swinging to Charleston, brass and piano, mechanical foley (relay, lift, doors), bells counted on the grid. Music about -9 dB under voice.
